Brickta Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Brickta respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Brickta application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Brickta, we may collect the following information:

  • Name and profile information.
  • Email address and account details.
  • Phone number if provided.
  • Profile photos and images uploaded by users.
  • Project information created within the app.
  • Messages and communication between users.
  • Location information when required for app features.
  • Device information used for notifications and app security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Brickta uses collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Connect homeowners, builders, and sellers.
  • Improve marketplace features and user experience.
  • Send important notifications and updates.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent misuse.

3. Account Information

Users are responsible for keeping their account information accurate and secure. You may update your profile information through the Brickta application.

4. Sharing of Information

Brickta does not sell your personal information.

Your information may be shared with other users when necessary for Brickta features, such as displaying builder profiles, seller information, project details, or communication required to complete platform activities.

We may share information when required by law or to protect the safety and security of Brickta and its users.

5. Messages and Communication

Messages sent through Brickta may be stored to provide messaging functionality, improve reliability, and maintain platform safety.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through chat unless necessary.

6. Payments and Financial Information

If payment features are introduced, payment information may be processed through secure third-party payment providers. Brickta does not store sensitive payment card information unless explicitly stated.

7. Data Security

Brickta uses reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting Brickta support or using the account deletion options provided in the app.

9. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Brickta account. Account deletion removes personal information according to our data retention practices and applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Brickta is not intended for children under the minimum age required to create an account in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Brickta may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Brickta support through the application.
