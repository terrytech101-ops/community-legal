We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is securely held and only handled by verified, third-party infrastructure providers to the extent necessary to deliver the application's core systems.

We implement strict technical and organizational safety measures to shield your project files and personal metadata against unauthorized access, loss, or manipulation.
